# Stereo & 5.1 Audio

Ports Crystal's SOUND option into the Gen 1 engine: MONO/STEREO music
with every sound effect routed through the chosen channel, plus a
surround mode that places each effect in the room around you.

## Install

1. Download `surround_audio-<version>.zip` from the
   [releases page](https://github.com/ShaneMcGovernIE/surround-audio/releases).
2. In the launcher: MODS → **Import mod .zip**.
3. Set the SOUND row in OPTIONS to STEREO or SURROUND.
