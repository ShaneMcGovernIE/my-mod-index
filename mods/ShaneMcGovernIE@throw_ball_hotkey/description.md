# Throw Ball Hotkey

In battle, press **T** on the main menu (FIGHT / PKMN / ITEM / RUN) to
instantly throw a POKé BALL. The ball is consumed and the turn is spent,
exactly as if you had picked it from the ITEM menu, and the full vanilla
catch animation plays.

**OPTIONS > THROW BALL** picks your preferred ball: POKé, GREAT, ULTRA or
MASTER. T throws the preferred ball when you own one, otherwise it falls
back to the first ball in your bag. In the Safari Zone, T throws a
SAFARI BALL from your safari counter instead.

With **Mods Hotkeys** installed, the T hotkey appears in OPTIONS > MODS
HOTKEYS and can be rebound to any key, button or combo.

## Install

1. Download `throw_ball_hotkey-1.1.0.zip` from the
   [releases page](https://github.com/ShaneMcGovernIE/throw_ball_hotkey/releases).
2. In the launcher: MODS → **Import mod .zip**.

With `"github": "ShaneMcGovernIE/throw_ball_hotkey"` set, the launcher's **Update**
and **Versions** buttons handle new releases from there.
